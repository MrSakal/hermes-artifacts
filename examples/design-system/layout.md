# Layout

- Content is one column of at most 720px of text; product grids go to 1080px.
- Gutters: `space-6` on phones, `space-8` from 768px.
- A product card is a photograph (4:5), the name in `title`, then one line of `small` in `ink-muted` with clay, glaze and size.
- The one clay button goes at the end of the reading order, never at the top of a card.
